City of Chicago

Citywide

Chicago

The nine neighborhoods below are the ones we work in most. We cover Chicago from city to suburbs, so if your block isn't listed, call and ask. Rob will tell you straight.

Chicago · Near Northwest Side

Service area

Wicker Park

Vintage two- and three-flats, frame cottages and storefront apartments around the six-corner crossing of Milwaukee, North and Damen.

HVAC here: boilers and radiators, basement furnaces, ductless where ducts won't fit.

Chicago · Near West Side

Service area

West Loop

Converted warehouse lofts, high-rise condos and restaurant kitchens around Fulton Market, about a mile and a half south of our shop.

HVAC here: steam in older lofts, fan coils in towers, kitchen make-up air.

Chicago · West Side

Home of our shop

West Town

Our shop is here, at 1225 N Cleaver St, among two- and three-flats, workers' cottages and new-build condos.

HVAC here: shared boilers, basement furnaces, condensers on garage roofs.

Chicago · North Side

Service area

Lincoln Park

Greystones, two-flats, courtyard buildings and lakefront high-rises, just north-east of our shop across the river.

HVAC here: boilers and radiators, furnaces, ductless for tight cottages.

Chicago · Lower West Side

Service area

Pilsen

Nineteenth-century brick two-flats and storefront buildings in the Pilsen Historic District, with 18th Street at its heart.

HVAC here: older boilers and radiators, furnaces in converted flats.

Chicago · Far North Side

Service area

Rogers Park

Lakefront neighborhood at the city's north edge, with courtyard apartment buildings and vintage condos around Loyola University.

HVAC here: central boilers in courtyard buildings, ductless and through-wall cooling.

Chicago · Near Northwest Side

Service area

Bucktown

North of Wicker Park, between Western Avenue and the Kennedy, with The 606 trail along its southern edge. Vintage frame homes beside new builds.

HVAC here: high-efficiency furnaces and central air, boilers in older homes.

Chicago · Northwest Side

Service area

Logan Square

Greystones and boulevard homes around the Illinois Centennial Monument, with brick two-flats on the side streets.

HVAC here: boilers and radiators in greystones, furnaces in frame houses.

Chicago · West Side

Service area

Humboldt Park

Brick two-flats and frame homes around the park, its lagoon and the field house, a short drive west of our shop along Division Street.

HVAC here: boilers in two-flats, forced-air furnaces, window-unit upgrades to ductless.
