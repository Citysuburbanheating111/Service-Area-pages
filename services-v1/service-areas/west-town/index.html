The permit record, honestly

Why you won't find us in the City's permit file

Chicago doesn't require a permit to repair, diagnose or maintain heating and cooling, which is most of what we do. The permit file measures construction, not service. So when a company waves a permit count at you, ask how much of their work is repair.

Questions

West Town HVAC, answered

Does the boiler in my two-flat need replacing, or can it be repaired?

Often it can be repaired. We diagnose first, and where the boiler has years left, we say so. Replacement is quoted only when the repair stops making sense.

How far is your shop from West Town?

We're in it. The shop is at 1225 N Cleaver St, half a block north of Division, and Rob runs the calls from there.

Can you cool a radiator-heated two-flat without ductwork?

Usually, yes. Radiator-heated flats have no ducts for central air, so cooling means ductless mini-splits, with the condenser on a garage roof or in the gangway.

Does HVAC repair in Chicago need a permit?

No. Chicago doesn't require a permit to repair, diagnose or maintain heating and cooling. Permits cover installs and construction, so a company's permit count says little about its repair work.
