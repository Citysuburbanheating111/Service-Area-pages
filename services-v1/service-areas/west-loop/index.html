How to choose

Five questions that sort West Loop HVAC companies

Ask them of anyone you call, including us.

  1. Q1

    Who diagnoses, and who quotes?

    If they're different people, ask who owns the outcome. Here it's Rob for both.

  2. Q2

    Is the technician on commission?

    Commission on replacements changes what "your furnace is done" means.

  3. Q3

    Does a permit count mean anything here?

    Not for repairs. Chicago doesn't permit HVAC repair or maintenance, so a permit count only measures installs.

  4. Q4

    What do the newest reviews say?

    Read the last 20, not the top 5. Look for names and specifics.

  5. Q5

    Will they tell you to keep it?

    Ask if the unit has years left. Our reviewers record hearing "yes".

The permit record, honestly

Why you won't find us in the City's permit file

Chicago doesn't require a permit to repair, diagnose or maintain heating and cooling, which is most of what we do. The permit file measures construction, not service. So when a company waves a permit count at you, ask how much of their work is repair.

Questions

West Loop HVAC, answered

Do I need a new furnace, or can mine be repaired?

Often it can be repaired. We diagnose first, and where the unit has years left, we say so. Replacement is quoted only when the repair stops making sense.

Do you work in West Loop high-rises?

Yes. Before a visit, high-rises usually want a certificate of insurance, a booked freight elevator and set working hours, so we book around your building's elevator and work hours. In towers that's often in-unit fan coils or air handlers on a building loop.

Can you service the steam boiler in my loft?

Yes. We service hot-water and steam boilers in vintage two-flats and converted lofts: pressure, circulators, zone valves and venting.

Does HVAC repair in Chicago need a permit?

No. Chicago doesn't require a permit to repair, diagnose or maintain heating and cooling. Permits cover installs and construction, so a company's permit count says little about its repair work.

What are your hours?

Our working hours are 24/7. Call (773) 238-3838.

No heat, no cooling, or just a question?

Have the make of your furnace, boiler or condenser to hand, and tell us what it's doing. In a high-rise, mention your building's work hours.
