What changes the price

The same repair costs differently in a Victorian house, a two-flat and a loft

We don't publish a price book, and we're not going to invent one here. What we can show you is what moves the number.

  1. 01

    Landmark district

    Much of Wicker Park is a Chicago Landmark District, so a new condenser or vent in view of the street can need Landmarks review.

  2. 02

    Boiler or forced air

    The big houses on Hoyne and Pierce, and many two-flats, still heat with a boiler and radiators, a different job from a furnace.

  3. 03

    Condo conversions

    Many Wicker Park two-flats are now condos, so one boiler can serve every unit and the owners agree the repair between them.

  4. 04

    Cooling a radiator house

    Victorian houses in the historic district were built for radiators, not ducts, so cooling usually means ductless mini-splits.

  5. 05

    Lofts above the shops

    Lofts over Milwaukee Avenue and Division run splits or rooftop units, so getting onto the roof sets the work.

  6. 06

    Repair or replace

    The biggest swing of all. Where the unit has years left, we say so.

The permit record, honestly

Why you won't find us in the City's permit file

Chicago doesn't require a permit to repair, diagnose or maintain heating and cooling, which is most of what we do. The permit file measures construction, not service. So when a company waves a permit count at you, ask how much of their work is repair.

01Does the old boiler in my Wicker Park house need replacing?

Often it doesn't. We diagnose first, and where the boiler has years left, we say so. Replacement is quoted only when repairs stop making sense.

02How far is your shop from Wicker Park?

Very close. The shop is at 1225 N Cleaver St, a fifth of a mile east of Ashland Avenue, just north of Division, and Rob runs the calls from there.

03Can you add air conditioning to a house heated by radiators?

Usually, yes. A radiator-heated house has no ducts for central air, so cooling usually means ductless mini-splits. In the landmark district, plan where the outdoor unit goes first.

04Does HVAC repair in Chicago need a permit?

No. Chicago doesn't require a permit to repair, diagnose or maintain heating and cooling. Permits cover installs and construction, so a company's permit count says little about its repair work.
