Chicago median
Median reported value of 81 central-furnace replacement permits in Chicago over 24 months. That's the whole project's construction value, not a quote. Yours depends on the factors below.
Factor · why it changes the work
01
Rooftop access
Condensers on loft and tower roofs mean roof hatches, ladders, and sometimes a crane for a replacement.
02
Building rules
High-rises want a certificate of insurance, a booked freight elevator and set working hours before anyone arrives.
03
The system you inherited
Steam and hot-water boilers in converted warehouses, fan coils in towers, forced air in newer mid-rises. Each is a different repair.
04
Concrete and exposed ceilings
Line sets and ductwork can't be hidden in a slab. In a loft they have to run where they look intentional.
05
Restaurant kitchens
Make-up air has to balance the hood. Work is scheduled around service on Randolph and in Fulton Market.
06
Repair or replace
The biggest swing of all. Where the unit has years left, we say so.
